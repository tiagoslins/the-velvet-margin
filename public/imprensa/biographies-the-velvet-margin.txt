BIOGRAPHIES — THE VELVET MARGIN

TIAGO LINS — Project lead, songwriter, music producer and executive coordinator
Songwriter, music producer and audio engineer trained in Audio Recording and Production at the Institute of Audio Research in New York. He also holds a degree in Social Sciences from the Federal University of São Carlos and created The Velvet Margin.

ARTHUR KRENA — Lead vocals
Singer whose musical journey began in childhood through his family environment and his father, a professional musician connected to blues and rock. He developed his vocal experience in church and also works as an illustrator and graphic designer.

DIEGO AQUINO — Bass
Bassist and music producer with more than two decades in São Paulo’s independent scene. Trained at Conservatório Souza Lima, his work is shaped by rhythmic precision and the influence of Black Music, soul and R&B.

YVES REMONT — Guitar
Guitarist, composer and music teacher active in São Paulo’s music scene, with experience in rock, Brazilian music and instrumental music. He is a member of Ki’MBANDA and contributes textures, riffs and harmonic language to The Velvet Margin.

RUBENS DE OLIVEIRA — Drums and percussion
Percussionist, drummer, composer and arranger trained at EMESP, the São Paulo Municipal School of Music, USP and UFMG. He is timpanist and head of percussion at the Theatro São Pedro Orchestra.

ANDERSON KAFÉ — Percussion
Percussionist from Vila Nova Cachoeirinha with nearly three decades in São Paulo’s scene. His work brings together forró pé de serra, maracatu, MPB, reggae, samba and Afro-Brazilian music.

TXAI ZERBETO SUARES SOUZA — Art direction and design
Art director, designer, copywriter and illustrator experienced in visual identities, campaigns, editorial projects and graphic materials. He leads the project’s visual identity, artwork and art direction.

DAYANNE SILVA — Administrative coordination and copyright
Professional experienced in cultural events, concerts and audiovisual projects. She supports documentation, internal processes, administrative coordination and copyright matters.
