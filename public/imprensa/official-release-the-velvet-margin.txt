OFFICIAL RELEASE — THE VELVET MARGIN

The Velvet Margin is an original album project created by Tiago Lins, a songwriter, music producer and audio engineer. Drawing from soul, blues and contemporary Black music, the work brings together up to ten tracks about memory, labor, identity, resistance, spirituality, hope and freedom.

The songs examine how historical structures remain present in everyday life and transform individual experiences into a collective narrative. The album begins with protest but does not end there: music becomes a space for memory, affirmation and belonging.

“The Velvet Margin: Production, Recording and Release of an Original Album” was approved by ProAC ICMS under project no. 56889, with a BRL 100,000 approved budget. Its planned outcomes include album production, digital release, a free public performance, a free independent music production workshop and audiovisual documentation.

The team includes Arthur Krena on lead vocals, Diego Aquino on bass, Yves Remont on guitar, Rubens de Oliveira on drums and percussion, Anderson Kafé on percussion, and professionals responsible for production, art direction and cultural management.
